A murder among the miniaturists of sixteenth-century Istanbul, told by a
different voice in every chapter: the painters, the dead man, a coin, the
color red itself.

What stays with me is the argument under the plot. The old masters believed
that a painter's style is a flaw and that the best work carries no signature.
The new manner coming from the West says the opposite. The book lets both
sides speak and does not hurry to pick one.

It is also the reason this project has its name: careful, patient work that
does not ask to be noticed.
