This is the demo site of [pamuk](https://github.com/ofkindness/pamuk), a static
blog starter built with SvelteKit.

Everything you see here comes from Markdown files in `sites/demo/content`.
Replace them with your own, edit `site.config.ts` and `theme.css`, and you
have a blog.
